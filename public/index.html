What decides the score

A fixed checklist, in code, the same every time. It never asks AI for anything, so the score is identical whether AI is on, off or broken. Five categories. Open one to see its checks.

What AI writes

When available, AI writes the commentary, the one-line verdict and the rewrite. It gets the finished score and cannot change it. Everything it writes is checked first. It can't:

  • state or imply a score
  • predict reach
  • put a number in a rewrite that your post didn't contain
  • say "as an AI"
  • use an em dash

It also writes one card, What it adds, under the roasts: whether your post gives a reader anything, with one line quoted from your post word for word. No quote, no card. A post under 40 words with an image gets no card, since the read cannot see the picture. Nothing on the card is part of the score.

It can still get a name or a detail wrong, so read a rewrite before you post it. Anything that fails is swapped for the checklist's version. The badge by the score says who wrote the commentary. It is not a verdict on whether your post is AI-written.

When it says no

A post about a death, serious illness, self-harm, abuse, violence or pregnancy loss is declined before anything else runs. No AI, no score, no cache. It errs toward declining on purpose.

What happens to what you paste

The post and any image go to Anthropic's API for the commentary. Here, nothing about your post is stored: not the post, not the image, not the AI's notes, not a rewrite. Every paste gets its own fresh read. Your browser keeps the last report in this tab's memory until the tab closes, so a reload does not lose it; that never leaves your browser. What is kept here is counters: an hourly per-IP limit that expires on its own, and tallies of scores and which checks fired, with no text in them. No accounts, no analytics on your text.
